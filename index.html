01Overview

Word Strike is an arcade game. This policy explains what information the app handles when you sign in with Google, play, and take part in the world leaderboard.

We never sell your data and show no ads. The only things other players can see are your callsign and your best score on the world leaderboard.

02What we collect

  • From Google Sign-In: your name, email address and Google account ID.
  • From you: the callsign (username) you choose.
  • Game data: your scores, tutorial progress and settings.
  • World leaderboard: your callsign, best score, level reached, words destroyed and when it was set.

03How we use it

To personalise the game (greeting you and showing your callsign), to remember your progress and preferences, and to rank your best score against other players on the world leaderboard.

04Where it's stored

Your profile, scores and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device, and are not included in cloud backups or transfers to a new phone.

Your world leaderboard entry is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). To make sure each entry belongs to a real player, Firebase Authentication also keeps your Google account ID, name and email. Your email is never shown to other players.

05Service providers

Sign-in is handled by Google Play services, and the leaderboard runs on Google Firebase. To keep the leaderboard fair, Firebase App Check uses Google Play Integrity to confirm that scores come from the genuine app; this checks the app and device, not your personal data.

Google's Privacy Policy applies to how Google processes this data. We don't share your data with anyone else.

06Ads & tracking

Word Strike contains no ads, analytics or third-party trackers.

07Permissions

Internet and network status, only for the world leaderboard (the game itself plays offline), and vibration for optional haptic feedback. No location, camera, microphone, contacts or biometric access.

08Children

If you are under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), please ask a parent or guardian before signing in.

09Your choices & account deletion

You can sign out at any time. To delete your account and all your data from inside the app:

  1. Open Word Strike and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete all data.
  3. Confirm your Google account. The app then removes your world leaderboard entry, deletes your Firebase account and erases everything on your device.

You can also email us to ask for your data to be deleted. Uninstalling the app removes the data on your device.

✉ Request deletion by email

Callsigns must not be offensive. You can report one from the world leaderboard (or by email), and we remove callsigns that break this rule.

10Retention

Your leaderboard entry is kept until you delete it. Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.

11Changes

If this policy changes, the app will ask you to review and accept it again.
